CLINICAL NLPONGOING RESEARCH

Turning patient language
into clinical meaning.

People rarely describe their health in the language of a medical terminology. I develop retrieval-augmented pipelines to connect patient narratives to RxNorm, SNOMED CT, and CPT-4 concepts.

My contribution
Hybrid retrieval, cross-encoder reranking, LLM-based selection, and text style transfer on multi-GPU HPC infrastructure.
Why it matters
More reliable language understanding is a foundation for useful patient-facing conversational systems.
